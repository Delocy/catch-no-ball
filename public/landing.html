The words are English. The meaning is not always what it sounds like.

"Can lor."
You hear: Great, yes!
Actually: a very reluctant yes.
"Confirm cannot one."
You hear: Maybe, let's see?
Actually: definitely not.
"Aiyah, siam it."
You hear: ...a word you don't know
Actually: skip it, avoid it.
